About Eendrag
Karakter · Styl · Trots, sedert 1961
Eendrag is a men's residence at Stellenbosch University, home to around 270 students known as Eendragters, or simply "Draggies." Established in 1961, the house was built around the idea of unity — its central courtyard and shared balconies were designed to bring residents together, and that spirit still shapes daily life here. The residence is organised into twelve sections, each with its own character and traditions, and residents compete against one another each year in the Intersection Competition across a range of sports and activities.
Beyond the buildings and traditions, Eendrag is built on the values in its motto — Character, Style, and Pride — and on a house culture that balances a strong social life with genuine academic support. Guided by the Primarius, Vice-Primarius, House Committee, and mentors, the house aims to give every resident a sense of belonging from their very first year, and a network of friendships that lasts well beyond it.
